Cancel this proposal?

You can cancel now and return to pay for this proposal within 48 hours of its creation.

  1. Keep your proposal number so you can retrieve the proposal and resume payment.
  2. Complete payment within 48 hours of creating the proposal. After that, you will need to generate a new quote.
  3. Generated proposals cannot be edited. To change your trip, plan or traveller details, create a new quote. The premium and proposal number may change.
