From trip details to take-off.

Five steps from trip details to travel cover.

  1. Step 1: Trip details

    Enter your destination, travel dates and who is travelling.

  2. Step 2: Choose a plan

    Compare available plans, coverage, benefits and prices, then select your plan.

  3. Step 3: Generate quote

    Review your selected plan, choose optional add-ons and complete each traveller’s personal, identity and beneficiary details to generate your quote.

  4. Step 4: Review quote

    Check the generated quote or proposal, including traveller details, selected add-ons and the total premium, before proceeding to payment.

  5. Step 5: Payment

    Complete payment, then receive your policy or Certificate of Cover (COC) and payment receipt.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common travel-cover questions. Your selected policy remains the source for the exact terms.

What does travel insurance cover?

Depending on the plan, benefits may include emergency medical expenses, travel disruption, baggage, and personal accident. Review the selected insurer’s benefits, limits, exclusions, and policy wording before purchasing.

Can I choose a single-trip or annual plan?

The search provides both options. A single-trip plan is intended for one journey; annual-plan eligibility and per-trip limits depend on the provider. Confirm the applicable terms when comparing plans.

Is this cover available for domestic travel?

Choose Domestic for travel within the Philippines, or International to select a country abroad. Available plans and benefits depend on the destination and provider. Cover is for air travel only.

Is COVID-19 covered?

Check the selected plan’s wording for any COVID-19 benefits, conditions, and exclusions. Do not assume that every available plan includes the same protection.

Are pre-existing medical conditions covered?

Providers may apply exclusions or specific conditions. Review the selected policy wording and confirm eligibility with the insurer before purchase.

What about pregnancy and adventure activities?

Pregnancy-related expenses and activities such as adventure sports can have specific exclusions or limits. Check the insurer’s terms for your intended activities and circumstances.

What is the difference between cancellation and interruption?

Cancellation relates to a trip that does not start; interruption relates to a trip that has already begun. Covered reasons, evidence requirements, and benefit limits are set by the selected policy.

What happens if my baggage is lost or delayed?

A plan may provide benefits for eligible baggage events. Keep the travel provider’s reports and supporting documents, then follow the insurer’s claim requirements.

What medical and evacuation benefits are available?

Medical treatment and emergency evacuation benefits vary by plan. Review the limits and assistance arrangements, including any requirement to contact the insurer before making arrangements.

Can I include my family?

Use the traveller control to describe your group. Eligibility, age limits, and family-plan availability depend on the provider. You can include up to nine travellers in a quote.

How do I make a claim or get emergency assistance?

Use the assistance and claims contacts on the issued policy or certificate. The insurer’s required documents and notification time limits apply.

Can I retrieve an existing proposal?

Use Retrieve proposal with your proposal reference and email address, or contact our team for help.
